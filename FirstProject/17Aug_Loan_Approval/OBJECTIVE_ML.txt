OBJECTIVE
Build, train, evaluate and test a classification model that predicts whether a loan application is likely to be
Approved or Rejected.
PROJECT OVERVIEW
Business Problem A bank receives loan applications from customers. Use historical application data to predict whether a
new application will be Approved or Rejected.
