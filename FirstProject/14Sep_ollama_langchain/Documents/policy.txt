INSTITUTE POLICY DOCUMENT

Course Withdrawal Policy

Students can withdraw from a course before the official
withdrawal deadline published by the institute.

Students should contact the academic office before withdrawing.

Withdrawal after the deadline may require special approval.


Attendance Policy

Students are expected to attend classes regularly.

Students should maintain at least 80 percent attendance.

Students who have attendance below the required level
may be contacted by the academic office.


Assignment Policy

Students must submit assignments before the stated deadline.

Late assignments may receive reduced marks unless
an extension has been approved.


Academic Integrity Policy

Students must complete academic work honestly.

Plagiarism, cheating and unauthorized copying are not permitted.

Academic misconduct may result in disciplinary action.
